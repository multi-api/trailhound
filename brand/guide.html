Search that stays on the trail.

A low-memory search engine with a Meilisearch-compatible API. This guide defines how it looks and how it speaks.

The idea

A hound finds what it is after with very little: it picks up a trail and does not let go. That is the product: it finds fast, takes little room and loses no data. The brand is plain and direct, with one warm accent on neutral surfaces.

Symbol

The head of a hound in profile, reduced to three shapes: head, ear and eye. The ear carries the accent colour and is the feature that identifies it.

Logo

Symbol and name in lowercase, set in Space Grotesk Bold. In running text the name is written "Trailhound", with a capital T.

Usage rules

  • Leave clear space around it equal to the width of the ear.
  • The symbol is ink with an orange ear, or snow with a light-orange ear. Do not recolour it.
  • Do not rotate it, distort it, or add a shadow or outline.
  • On photos or backgrounds other than snow, ink or trail, use the icon with its own background.

Colour

Three colours do nearly all the work: ink, snow and trail. The rest are supporting colours. The values live in tokens.css.

Contrast

  • Trail (#D9652B) on snow does not reach text contrast: use it for shapes, not for small type.
  • For accent text and buttons on light, use Trail text (#A8440F) with white type.
  • On dark, the accent is Trail light (#F08A4B) with ink type.

Typography

Three open-licence (OFL) families. The admin panel uses system fonts so that self-hosted installs load nothing external.
